About

The Only Consultant
Qualified in Both

Dr Schaida Schirwani is the UK's first and only Consultant dual-qualified in Clinical Genetics and Dermatology — a unique combination that allows her to bridge two specialties that are deeply intertwined, particularly in the diagnosis and management of genodermatoses, hereditary skin cancer syndromes, and rare genetic skin disorders.

She holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBChB), a Master of Science in Medical Genetics, and is a Member of the Royal College of Physicians (MRCP). She practices within the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, where she also leads cancer genetics provision for the Dorset area.

An active researcher with over 32 peer-reviewed publications and 193 academic citations, Dr Schirwani is passionate about translating genomic science into meaningful clinical outcomes — for patients, their families, and the next generation of clinicians.

Clinical Expertise

Areas of Specialisation

A unique dual-specialty perspective covering the full breadth of genetic and dermatological conditions.

01

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Genetic assessment of intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, developmental delay, and associated rare syndromes.

02

Cancer Predisposition Syndromes

Hereditary cancer risk assessment including BRCA, Lynch syndrome, multiple endocrine neoplasia, and familial polyposis.

03

Dermatology & Genodermatoses

Diagnosis and management of genetic skin disorders, hereditary skin cancer syndromes, and conditions at the intersection of genetics and dermatology.

04

Dysmorphology

Clinical evaluation of congenital anomalies and dysmorphic features to identify their underlying genetic basis.

05

Rare & Undiagnosed Conditions

Diagnosis of ultra-rare monogenic disorders, chromosomal anomalies, and conditions without a prior diagnosis — including ASXL3-related syndrome.

06

Genomic Medicine

Whole genome and exome sequencing interpretation, variant classification, and translation of genomic findings into clinical management.
