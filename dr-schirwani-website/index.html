About

Dedicated to Genetics
with Compassion

Dr Schaida Schirwani is a Consultant Clinical Geneticist at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, where she practices within the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service. She holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBChB), a Master of Science in Medical Genetics, and is a Member of the Royal College of Physicians (MRCP).

Her clinical work encompasses a broad range of genetic conditions including neurodevelopmental disorders, dysmorphology, autism spectrum conditions, and cancer predisposition syndromes, with a specific focus on cancer genetics services for the Dorset area.

Dr Schirwani is an active researcher with over 32 peer-reviewed publications and more than 193 academic citations. She is committed to translating genetic research into meaningful clinical outcomes for patients and their families.

Clinical Expertise

Areas of Specialisation

Providing expert assessment and management across a wide spectrum of genetic conditions, with a patient-centred and evidence-based approach.

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Assessment and diagnosis of genetic causes underlying intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, global developmental delay, and rare syndromes.

Cancer Predisposition Syndromes

Specialist cancer genetics for the Dorset area, including hereditary breast and ovarian cancer, Lynch syndrome, and multiple endocrine neoplasia.

Dysmorphology

Evaluation and diagnosis of congenital anomalies and structural differences, identifying their genetic basis and implications for patients and families.

Rare Genetic Conditions

Diagnosis and management of ultra-rare monogenic conditions including ASXL3-related syndrome and other chromosomal and single-gene disorders.

Genetic Counselling

Compassionate guidance for individuals and families navigating genetic diagnoses, inheritance risks, family planning, and reproductive options.

Genomic Medicine

Interpretation of whole genome and exome sequencing, genomic variant classification, and translating genomic findings into clinical management.
