About

Genetics with Expertise
and Empathy

Dr Schaida Schirwani is a Consultant Clinical Geneticist at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, practising within the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service. She holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (MBChB), a Master of Science in Medical Genetics, and is a Member of the Royal College of Physicians (MRCP).

Her clinical work spans a broad range of genetic conditions, with particular expertise in neurodevelopmental disorders, dysmorphology, autism spectrum conditions, and hereditary cancer syndromes — including specialist cancer genetics provision for the Dorset area.

An active researcher with over 32 peer-reviewed publications and 193 academic citations, Dr Schirwani is passionate about translating cutting-edge genomic science into meaningful clinical care for individuals and their families.

Clinical Expertise

Areas of Specialisation

Evidence-based, compassionate assessment across the full spectrum of genetic conditions.

01

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Genetic assessment of intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, developmental delay, and associated rare syndromes.

02

Cancer Predisposition Syndromes

Hereditary cancer risk assessment for Dorset, including BRCA, Lynch syndrome, multiple endocrine neoplasia and familial polyposis.

03

Dysmorphology

Clinical evaluation of congenital anomalies, dysmorphic features, and structural differences to identify their underlying genetic cause.

04

Rare & Undiagnosed Conditions

Diagnosis of ultra-rare monogenic disorders including ASXL3-related syndrome, chromosomal anomalies, and conditions without prior diagnosis.

05

Genomic Medicine

Whole genome and exome sequencing interpretation, variant classification, and translation of genomic findings into clinical management.

06

Genetic Counselling

Compassionate guidance on inheritance, recurrence risks, family planning, and reproductive options for individuals and families.
