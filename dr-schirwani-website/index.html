About Dr Schirwani

A distinguished academic clinician with extensive NHS and research experience.

Dr Schaida Schirwani is a Consultant Clinical Geneticist at the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service, University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust. With advanced training in medical genetics and general internal medicine (MRCP), she brings a broad clinical perspective to every patient encounter.

Her clinical work encompasses assessment, diagnosis, and management of individuals and families with a wide range of genetic conditions: from complex neurodevelopmental presentations in children to rare dysmorphic syndromes and hereditary conditions.

Dr Schirwani is an active researcher with over 32 peer-reviewed publications, including contributions to GeneReviews (NIH). She is committed to translating cutting-edge genomic science into meaningful clinical care.

In private practice, she offers timely access to specialist genetic assessment, detailed counselling, and coordination with other specialists, supporting families through a complex and often emotional journey.

Areas of Expertise

Specialist assessment and genetic counselling across a broad spectrum of conditions, with particular depth in rare and complex presentations.

Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Assessment of developmental delay, intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, ADHD, and complex behavioural presentations with an underlying genetic aetiology.

Dysmorphology

Clinical evaluation of individuals with unusual physical features, structural differences, or congenital anomalies to identify underlying genetic syndromes.

Rare Genetic Conditions

Diagnosis and management of rare, ultra-rare, and novel genetic conditions, including ASXL3-related (Bainbridge-Ropers) syndrome and other recently characterised genomic disorders.

Genetic Counselling

Patient-centred counselling to help individuals and families understand the implications of a genetic diagnosis, recurrence risks, and reproductive options.

Genomic Interpretation

Interpretation of whole-exome and whole-genome sequencing results, chromosomal microarray, and gene panels, translating complex genomic data into clear clinical guidance.

Paediatric Genetics

Holistic assessment of children referred with suspected genetic syndromes, including joint clinics with paediatric radiology, developmental paediatrics, and metabolic medicine.

What to Expect

Private consultations provide timely access to specialist genetics expertise with a thorough, unhurried approach tailored to your needs.

01

Initial Consultation

A comprehensive genetic assessment including detailed personal and family history, clinical examination where appropriate, and a thorough review of all previous investigations.

02

Diagnostic Testing

Arrangement of targeted or broad-based genetic testing including chromosomal microarray, gene panels, and exome sequencing through accredited UK laboratories.

03

Results and Counselling

A dedicated results appointment to discuss findings clearly and fully, including implications for the individual, siblings, and other family members, with a written summary provided.

04

Specialist Coordination

Liaison with other paediatric and adult specialists, referral to NHS services where appropriate, and coordination of a multi-disciplinary care plan when required.

05

Follow-Up and Support

Ongoing follow-up appointments as new genetic information emerges, plus written reports suitable for schools and other services.

06

Second Opinion

Expert independent review of previous genetic assessments, variant reclassifications, and diagnostic uncertainty, providing clarity when families have unresolved questions.

GP or specialist referral welcome. Self-referrals are also accepted. Most private health insurers recognise Dr Schirwani as a specialist; please check with your insurer before booking. A full written report is provided following every appointment.
