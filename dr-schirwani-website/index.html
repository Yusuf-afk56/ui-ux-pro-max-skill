About

A leading voice in clinical genetics

Dr. Schaida Schirwani is a Consultant Clinical Geneticist at the Wessex Clinical Genetics Service, University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust. With qualifications spanning medicine (MBChB), a postgraduate masters in Medical Genetics (MSc), and membership of the Royal College of Physicians (MRCP), she brings exceptional breadth of expertise to her patients.

Dr. Schirwani covers cancer genetics across the Dorset area and has established herself as a highly cited researcher in neurodevelopmental disorders, dysmorphology, disability studies, and autism spectrum conditions.

Her patient-centred approach combines clinical precision with compassionate communication, ensuring individuals and families receive thorough, accessible explanations of complex genetic conditions.

"Understanding your genetics is the first step to truly personalised, empowered healthcare."

Areas of Expertise

Specialist clinical genetics services

Offering comprehensive genetic assessment, diagnosis, and counselling across a broad spectrum of inherited and acquired conditions.

Cancer Genetics

Risk assessment and genetic testing for hereditary cancer syndromes including BRCA-related, Lynch syndrome, and rare cancer predisposition conditions. Covers the Dorset region.

  • Hereditary breast & ovarian cancer
  • Lynch syndrome
  • Rare cancer predisposition syndromes
  • TP53 & FLCN pathogenic variants

Neurodevelopmental Genetics

Expert assessment of genetic causes underlying intellectual disability, autism spectrum disorder, and developmental delay — with a particular focus on ASXL3-related syndrome.

  • Autism spectrum disorder (ASD)
  • Intellectual disability & developmental delay
  • ASXL3-related syndrome
  • Genomic sequencing & interpretation

Dysmorphology & Rare Disease

Clinical assessment and molecular diagnosis of rare genetic syndromes and chromosomal conditions, including chromosomal microarray analysis and whole-genome sequencing.

  • Rare genetic syndrome diagnosis
  • Chromosomal microarray analysis
  • 1p32 microdeletion syndrome
  • Paediatric genetic referrals

Genetic Counselling

Compassionate, thorough counselling to help patients and families understand genetic test results, inheritance patterns, and implications for family planning.

  • Pre- & post-test counselling
  • Family risk & inheritance patterns
  • Variant of uncertain significance (VUS) interpretation
  • Reproductive genetics
